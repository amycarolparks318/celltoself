I’m Not Ready to Write This

1/21/26

I’m not ready to write this first post.

At least, not the one people might expect. I know there’s plenty to say, but that would mean taking apart the compartments I’ve built so carefully.

What if they’re the only reason I function, survive, and keep moving forward without falling apart? Oh God, what if I’m nothing without them?

Emotional detachment and dissociation have been my favorite coping mechanisms for a long time. I’m practically an expert now.

I just don’t know if I’m ready to let that go.

So this is me showing up anyway. Not with answers. Not with a breakthrough. Just being honest about where I’m starting. I’m learning to sit with discomfort instead of filing it away for later. I’m getting there.

I’m not ready to write this.
But I’m writing it anyway.

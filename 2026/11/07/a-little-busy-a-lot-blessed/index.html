A Little Busy, A Lot Blessed

Sorry it's been a minute! I've been absolutely swamped, but with all good things! Work is great! I created some software that's been instrumental to our project, and my website design company is launching this week!

My sponsee did her 5th Step last week, and we're working on Steps 6 and 7 today. I'm chairing the AA meeting tonight, and I had the opportunity to speak at another meeting a couple of weeks ago.

My boyfriend treats me like a princess and is so good to me. ❤️ AND GUESS WHAT?????? My oldest son, the one in the Marines? WE'RE TEXTING NOW!!! He even said maybe sometime soon we could have a phone call or a visit! 🥹❤️

I don't have time to write a ton right now, so I figured I'd share my latest blog post, The Other Side of a Second Chance.

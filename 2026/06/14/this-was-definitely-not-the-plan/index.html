This Was Definitely Not the Plan

**3/15/26** <-ACTUAL DATE WRITTEN

Hi guys!

I know I have been way too quiet lately. Life has been absolutely insane, but I am good and still clean.

So… I ended up getting kicked out of the halfway house. It was ridiculous. I will tell you guys more about that later, but long story short, I moved in with the guy I am seeing now temporarily. Now, before everyone starts freaking out, living there was NEVER the plan. I am now staying with my sponsor but temporarily again.

Anyway, so my boss had to unexpectedly go back home and left the company. He asked me if I wanted to come to Arkansas and work with his new company.

Guys… I just DON’T KNOW.

Everything is happening so fast, and for the first time in my life, I am actually trying to slow down and make the right decisions instead of just jumping into something because it feels good. With work, with men, with everything….

But wait… because apparently my life needed another plot twist.

The Next Chapter Coding Boot Camp applications are due on 3/20, and I am applying!

For those who don’t know, it is a software development boot camp specifically for people who have been incarcerated. They pay you $700 a week to attend school for 15 weeks.

It is incredibly competitive, so I need all the prayers, good vibes, and positive thoughts you can send my way.

More soon. I promise.

Love you all!
